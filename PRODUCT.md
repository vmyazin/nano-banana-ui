# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Scene Assembly serves creators who generate, edit, and compose visual media across multiple AI providers. They use it as a working studio: selecting the right engine, supplying prompts and references, tuning supported controls, and keeping useful outputs available in the browser.

## Product Purpose

Scene Assembly provides one provider-neutral workspace for image and video generation. It helps creators compare capabilities, move between generation modes, and manage prompts, references, jobs, and results without learning a different interface for every provider. Success means the interface stays out of the way while making each model's options and state clear.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, and moderately energetic. The product should feel capable and creative without becoming loud or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Cyberpunk or gaming interfaces whose glow, ornament, or motion competes with the work.
- Generic AI-product styling built around purple gradients and decorative color.
- Sterile enterprise dashboards that erase the product's creative energy.

## Design Principles

- Keep the generated work central: controls support the creative task rather than competing for attention.
- Make provider differences legible inside one consistent interaction system.
- Use color to establish hierarchy and state, with moderate energy instead of constant intensity.
- Favor familiar, precise controls so creators can move quickly across image and video workflows.
- Preserve local-first clarity: explain what is stored in the browser, what is temporary, and what requires a provider credential.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific conformance target is required. Preserve established platform accessibility behavior, including keyboard operation, visible focus, readable text, and reduced-motion handling where motion is present.
