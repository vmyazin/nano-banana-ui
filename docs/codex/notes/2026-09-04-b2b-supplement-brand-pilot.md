## Business hypotheses discussed

Potential customers are small creative agencies managing recurring client campaigns, or in-house consumer-brand teams producing recurring product content. Sell a repeatable path from brief to approved assets. Potential paid value includes reusable production recipes, brand context, client/project organization, review and approvals, shared connections with permissions and spending limits, and cost per approved asset. Storage alone is not the proposed main paid benefit.

A $99–$199 monthly workspace subscription with a small included team and customer-owned provider keys was suggested only as a price to test. No pricing or willingness to pay has been validated. Provider charges would remain separate initially. Do not expand the approved personal-account release with team features merely because these ideas were discussed.

## Concrete pilot opportunity

The user has a friend whose company sells a daily supplement. Content production is mostly in-house, supplemented by agencies. They have not found an agency they love. The user identified creative quality and understanding the brand as the main frustrations.

This suggests testing brand fidelity and usable creative output, not merely speed or quantity. It does not establish that the app can replace agency strategy or creative direction.

## Proposed small pilot

Use one supplement, one real campaign brief, actual product photography/packaging and approved marketing copy. Preserve label accuracy; do not invent health claims or testimonials. Collect examples the creative lead likes and dislikes, with reasons. Develop three distinct creative directions; let the team choose one before scaling variations. A suggested deliverable is three image concepts and one short video, subject to the real brief.

Compare against their current process: time spent, generation cost, revision cycles, assets approved and actually published. Then let their team attempt a second brief with less assistance. Repeat use and eventual payment are stronger signals than friendly praise. If success depends on the founder supplying all creative judgment, it validates a service rather than self-serve software.

Potential later feature: a saved brand context containing approved references, visual preferences, rejected approaches and successful generation settings. Test this manually before building it.
